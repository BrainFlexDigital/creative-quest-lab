Reset All Progress?

This will erase all earned stars, completed missions, player name, and saved artwork or creations in My Creations. This action cannot be undone.

Save Creative Quest Lab for Offline Play

On iPhone & iPad Safari, save this game to your Home Screen so your child can open and play offline anytime:

1. Tap the Safari Share icon (square with upward arrow).
2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
3. Tap Add — launch the Quest Lab icon anytime, even without internet!
